Privacy Policy

Version 1 — 17 November 2026 — Shivam Staff App

1. Ye policy kis baare me hai

Ye app (Shivam Staff App) dukan/company ke kaam ke liye hai — attendance, duty, tour tracking, hisab aur alerts. Ye policy saaf-saaf batati hai ki app aapka kya data leta hai, kyun leta hai, kahan rakhta hai, aur kya KABHI nahi leta. Data collect karne ka base aapka employment hai — aap company ke liye kaam karte ho, isliye kaam se juda data company rakhti hai (app me consent dialog me iski manzoori tick karke di jati hai).

2. Kya data collect hota hai

  • Account: naam, mobile number, role (worker / sales / manager), kaam ki city.
  • Location: GPS aur network se aapki position — dukan/tour tracking, app-open gate (dukan ke 100 meter area ka check).
  • Mobile tower info: kis tower se phone juda hai (tower id — mcc-mnc-lac-cid), signal, aur usi se andaza ki city kaunsi hai. Ye GPS ke bina bhi chalta hai taaki internet/location band hone par bhi pata rahe.
  • Battery: battery % aur charging on/off — taaki pata chale tracking ruki to phone band thi ya kuch aur.
  • Motion hint (chalne/haath hilne ka sensor signal) — sirf battery bachane ke liye, GPS bewajah on na ho.
  • Duty punches: aane/jaane ka time, tour me mile visit log (kahan kitni der ruke), sales ka hisab (bills, diye paise, note) — ye business record hai.
  • App usage (sirf worker aur sales, sirf dukan ke time): kaun si app kitni der khuli rahi — sirf MINUTES ka total. Screen ka content, aapki chats, photos — kuch nahi dekha jata.

3. Kya KABHI collect nahi hota

  • Aapki photos, videos, files, contacts — kabhi nahi.
  • Aapke messages, WhatsApp ki chats, calls ki recording — kabhi nahi.
  • Browser me kya dekha — nahi (sirf "Chrome 1h 10m" jaisa total time, wo bhi sirf dukan ke time).
  • Dukan se bahar ka app usage — nahi. Personal time track nahi hota.
  • Microphone/camera — kabhi on nahi hote.

4. Data kahan jata hai aur kaun dekhta hai

Data company ke Google Firebase (Google Cloud) account par store hota hai. Ye dekh sakta hai: sirf management/boss (manager dashboard) — presence, tour, alerts aur hisab ke records. Koi teesri company ko becha nahi jata, advertising use nahi hota.

5. Permission kyun chahiye

  • Location (foreground + background): app khulne par gate + dukan par presence + tour tracking. Background taaki app band ho to bhi trail bane (boss ka order: app khule ya na khule, duty ka pata chale).
  • Notification: duty/alerts ki jankari, tracking chalu hone ki soochi.
  • Motion (Activity recognition): chalne ka signal mile to GPS turant on ho, khade rehne par band rahe — battery bachat hai.
  • Battery optimization exemption: phone ka battery-saver tracking ko na roke.
  • Usage access (optional, worker/sales): upar likhe app-usage totals ke liye — ye Android ka apna record hai, hum bas minutes padhte hain.

6. Fake GPS / location game

Agar koi fake-location app se galat jagah bhejne ki koshish ho, app usko pakad kar record karti hai (mock flag, GPS vs tower mismatch). Ye record sirf management dikh sakta hai.

7. Data kitne din rakha jata hai

Jab tak aap company me kaam karte ho. Kaam khatam hone par management apni policy se records rakh/saf kar sakti hai. Data delete karane ki request seedhe management ko do.

8. Consent aur usko hatana

Pehli baar app kholte hi permissions + ye policy dikhti hai — checkbox tick karne par aap terms & conditions aur data-use ki manzoori dete ho (kyunki aap company ke liye kaam karte ho). Permission phone settings se band kar do to app ke tracking wale features bhi band ho jate hain (app ye khud pakad leti hai). Koi sawal ho to seedhe management/dukan malik se baat karo.

9. Policy badalne par

Naya data collect karne se pehle policy update hoga aur app me dobara consent dialog aa sakta hai. Is page par hamesha latest version aur date likhi hoti hai (abhi: version 1, 17 November 2026).

Ye policy app ke andar bhi hai (offline bhi khulti hai) aur is hosted page se same hai. Koi confusion ho to management se puchho — likha sab kuch wahi hai jo app asal me karta hai.
